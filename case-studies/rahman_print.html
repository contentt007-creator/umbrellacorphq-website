About the Client

Rahman Corporation is a multi-vertical trading and import-export firm headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh. With a workforce of over 70 staff, the company operates across technology hardware distribution, commodity trading, and international procurement — serving both domestic corporate clients and overseas buyers.

When our engagement began, Rahman Corporation had built solid offline relationships over years of operation but had no structured digital presence, no brand identity, and no systematic method for reaching international buyers or generating inbound leads. Our partnership — now spanning over two years — has fundamentally transformed how the business acquires customers, builds credibility, and expands into global markets.

The relationship has also produced one of the most exciting consumer-facing outcomes of our portfolio: the Warrior brand of technology hardware, giving Rahman direct-to-market capability in Bangladesh's growing tech retail space.

The Challenge

  • 01

    Zero Digital Presence

    Despite years in operation, Rahman Corporation had no website, no social media, and no searchable digital footprint. International buyers and procurement managers researching suppliers could not find them.

  • 02

    No Brand Identity

    The company operated without a cohesive brand — no logo, no colour palette, no brand voice. This created a credibility gap when approaching international trade partners and large domestic buyers.

  • 03

    No Lead Generation System

    All business came through personal networks and referrals. There was no pipeline management, no inbound marketing funnel, and no outbound prospecting system — making revenue planning highly unpredictable.

  • 04

    No International Reach

    Rahman's import-export capability was largely theoretical — the firm had ambitions for international trade but lacked the digital infrastructure, brand credibility, and outreach systems to connect with overseas buyers systematically.

  • 05

    Untapped Audience Segments

    Significant market segments — including government procurement, SME technology buyers, and direct consumers — were entirely unaddressed. Rahman's commercial potential exceeded what their existing outreach could ever capture.

The Warrior Brand

One of the defining outcomes of our long-term partnership with Rahman Corporation is the creation and launch of Warrior — a locally positioned technology hardware brand designed to compete directly in Bangladesh's fast-growing retail and SME technology market. Built on Rahman's existing supply chain and import capability, Warrior gives the corporation a direct consumer and business-to-business technology channel with significantly higher margin per unit compared to wholesale commodity trading.
