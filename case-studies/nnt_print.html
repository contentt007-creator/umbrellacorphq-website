About the Client

NNT Bag Manufacturing is one of Dhaka's established bag manufacturers, operating with a workforce of over 100 skilled staff. The company produces a broad range of bags — from corporate giveaway bags and school backpacks to fashion-forward carry-alls — serving both wholesale and retail markets across Bangladesh.

Despite holding strong manufacturing capability and competitive pricing, NNT had virtually no digital footprint, no coherent brand identity, and no structured sales development process. The business relied entirely on word-of-mouth and offline relationships for lead generation. Our engagement began in February 2026 with a mandate to transform their market position from the ground up.

The Challenge

  • 01

    Zero Digital Presence

    No website, no social media profiles, no Google Business listing. The company was effectively invisible to online buyers and procurement managers searching for bag manufacturers in Dhaka.

  • 02

    No Brand Identity

    NNT lacked a logo, brand guidelines, visual language, or any consistent communication style. This made it impossible to present professionally to corporate buyers and export partners.

  • 03

    No Lead Generation System

    All sales came through personal referrals. There was no pipeline, no CRM, no inbound channel, and no outbound strategy. Revenue was entirely relationship-dependent and unpredictable.

  • 04

    No International Reach

    Despite competitive manufacturing costs and quality output, NNT had never successfully engaged an international buyer. Export potential was entirely untapped.

  • 05

    Untapped Audience Segments

    The consumer (B2C) market for branded bags remained completely unexplored. No direct-to-consumer brand, no e-commerce presence, and no awareness of the B2C opportunity the factory was positioned to serve.

The Bagora Story

One of the most significant outcomes of our engagement with NNT was the identification and launch of a B2C consumer brand: Bagora. Where NNT had been entirely focused on wholesale and corporate manufacturing, our market research revealed a clear appetite among Bangladeshi consumers for a quality-first, locally-manufactured bag brand with strong digital branding.

We conceptualised the Bagora brand from scratch — naming, visual identity, packaging, and digital storefront — all built on NNT's existing manufacturing capability. The Facebook page and website launched as part of Phase 3, allowing NNT to capture direct-to-consumer margin for the first time. Early response to the launch campaign exceeded projections, with the page accumulating significant organic engagement within the first weeks of operation.

Bagora represents a new revenue vertical for NNT — one that operates alongside the core B2B business and offers substantially higher margin per unit.
