What We Faced

The Challenge

01 01

No Brand Identity

NNT had no cohesive visual identity, no brand guidelines, and no professional materials to present to buyers. Every sales interaction started from zero credibility.

02 02

Zero Digital Presence

No website, no active social media, no search visibility. Potential buyers searching for Dhaka bag manufacturers could not find NNT, resulting in missed pipeline at every stage.

03 03

No Lead Generation System

Lead acquisition was entirely word-of-mouth and referral. There was no repeatable, scalable mechanism to bring in new B2B prospects month after month.

04 04

No International Reach

Despite holding strong manufacturing capability, NNT had no strategy, materials, or positioning to approach export markets or international wholesale buyers.

05 05

Untapped Audience Segments

The direct-to-consumer opportunity sat completely dormant. NNT's own manufacturing infrastructure could support a consumer brand — but that vision had never been activated.
