SEO for Bangladesh Businesses: Why Most Local Sites Rank on Page 10

The exact mistakes keeping Bangladesh businesses invisible on Google — and the proven fixes that drive consistent organic traffic without spending a taka on ads.

Search for almost any product or service in Bangladesh and you'll find the same pattern: the top results are dominated by a handful of businesses while hundreds of competitors languish on pages 5, 8, and 10. Most Bangladesh businesses have a website. Very few of them are doing anything to make it visible.

That's both a problem and an opportunity. The barrier to page-one rankings in Bangladesh is lower than almost anywhere else in the world. The competition isn't doing the basics — which means if you do the basics well, you win. This guide covers exactly what those basics are, why they're being ignored, and how to fix them systematically.

The Bangladesh SEO reality: Most Bangladeshi businesses that rank on Google did very little deliberately. They rank by default — because their competitors did even less. With structured SEO effort, any business in Dhaka, Chittagong, or Sylhet can dominate local search within 60–90 days for low-competition keywords.

The Bangladesh SEO Opportunity Nobody Is Taking

Bangladesh's digital economy is growing at an extraordinary rate. Internet penetration crossed 50% in 2024 and continues climbing. Mobile internet costs have dropped dramatically, bringing hundreds of thousands of new search users online every month. Bangladeshi consumers are increasingly searching online before making purchasing decisions — for everything from restaurants and real estate to garment sourcing and professional services.

Yet the overwhelming majority of Bangladesh businesses treat their website as a digital business card — something to show people, not something that actively generates business. They pay for web design, ignore SEO, and then wonder why the website "doesn't work."

The data tells the story clearly: the top organic result on Google gets approximately 28% of all clicks. Position 2 gets 15%. By page 2, click-through rates fall below 1%. Your website being on page 10 is functionally the same as not having a website at all.

Why 90% of Bangladesh Businesses Fail at SEO

Having audited dozens of Bangladeshi business websites, the same patterns appear repeatedly. These are the root causes of SEO failure in the Bangladesh market:

  • No keyword strategy whatsoever. The website was built without a single thought given to what potential customers are actually searching for. Pages are titled with the company name rather than the service. The homepage says "Welcome to ABC Company" instead of "Digital Marketing Agency in Dhaka."
  • Slow, unoptimised websites on cheap shared hosting. Bangladesh has 91% mobile internet penetration, yet most local business websites take 8–12 seconds to load on mobile. Google penalises slow sites heavily in rankings, and visitors abandon pages that take more than 3 seconds to load.
  • No Google Business Profile. For local searches like "web design company Dhaka" or "accountant Gulshan," Google's Local Pack (the map results at the top of the page) drives massive traffic. Most Bangladesh businesses haven't even claimed their free Google Business Profile.
  • Duplicate or thin content. Many Bangladesh business websites copy-paste content from competitors or from templates, giving Google no reason to rank them above the original source. Others have pages with fewer than 200 words — not enough to establish relevance for any keyword.
  • Zero backlinks. Google treats links from other websites as votes of confidence. Most Bangladesh business websites have never been mentioned or linked to by any other site, which means Google has no third-party signal that they're authoritative or trustworthy.
  • English-only content in a predominantly Bangla-speaking market. Millions of Bangladeshi users search in Bangla, especially on mobile. A business that only has English content misses an enormous segment of local search traffic.

The good news: all of these problems are fixable. Here is the systematic fix for each one.

Fix #1: Claim and Optimise Your Google Business Profile

If you have a physical location or serve customers in a specific area, your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the single highest-impact SEO action you can take in an afternoon — and it's completely free.

How to Set Up and Optimise Your Google Business Profile

  1. Claim your listing at business.google.com. Search for your business — it may already exist as an unverified listing. Claim it and complete the verification process (usually via postcard or phone).
  2. Fill every field completely. Business name (use your exact legal/trading name — don't stuff keywords), address, phone number, website URL, business hours (including holiday hours), and business category. Incomplete profiles rank lower than complete ones.
  3. Write a keyword-rich business description. You have 750 characters. Use them. Include your primary service, your location (Dhaka, Chittagong, etc.), and what makes you different. Example: "Full-service digital marketing agency in Gulshan, Dhaka. We help Bangladesh businesses grow with SEO, social media marketing, and paid advertising."
  4. Add professional photos. Businesses with photos receive 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks. Upload your interior, exterior, team, and product/service photos. Aim for at least 10 high-quality images.
  5. Collect Google reviews actively. Reviews are a major ranking factor for the Local Pack. Ask every satisfied customer to leave a Google review. Make it easy — send them the direct review link. Respond to every review, positive or negative, professionally and promptly.
  6. Post updates weekly. Google Business Profile has a "Posts" feature — use it. Share new services, offers, blog posts, and events. Regular posting signals that your business is active and engaged.

Local Pack dominance: In Dhaka, a business with a fully optimised Google Business Profile, 20+ reviews averaging 4.5 stars, and regular posts will appear in the Local Pack for dozens of relevant searches — often above competitors with older, established websites that have ignored their profile entirely.

Fix #2: Target the Right Keywords for Bangladesh

Keyword strategy for Bangladesh businesses requires a slightly different approach than global SEO. The search volumes are lower, the competition is lower, and there's a significant Bangla-language search segment that most businesses ignore entirely.

The Bangladesh Keyword Framework

  • Location + service keywords: "digital marketing agency Dhaka," "web design Chittagong," "accountant Gulshan," "garment manufacturer Bangladesh." These high-intent local keywords should be your primary targets.
  • Service keywords without location: "best digital marketing agency Bangladesh," "affordable SEO services Bangladesh." These are slightly more competitive but still very achievable for local businesses.
  • Problem/question keywords: "how to promote business online in Bangladesh," "why is my website not ranking Google Bangladesh." These lower-competition, long-tail keywords attract searchers early in the buying journey.
  • Bangla keywords: Use Google Keyword Planner to research Bangla-script search terms. Many product categories have substantial Bangla search volume — especially in consumer goods, food, healthcare, and local services.

Free Keyword Research Tools

  • Google Search Console — shows what keywords your site is already appearing for (even if you're not ranking well). Free and invaluable.
  • Google Keyword Planner — free with a Google Ads account. Shows search volumes and competition for any keyword in Bangladesh.
  • Ubersuggest (free tier) — Neil Patel's tool gives keyword ideas, volume estimates, and difficulty scores.
  • Google's autocomplete and "People Also Ask" — type your core service into Google and observe what suggestions appear. These are real searches from real users in Bangladesh.

Fix #3: On-Page SEO Fundamentals

On-page SEO refers to the optimisations you make directly on your website pages to help Google understand what each page is about. Most Bangladesh business websites score near zero on on-page SEO fundamentals. Here is the complete checklist:

Title Tags

The title tag is the clickable headline that appears in Google search results. It's one of the strongest on-page ranking signals. Every page needs a unique, keyword-rich title tag of 50–60 characters. Format: Primary Keyword — Secondary Keyword | Brand Name. Example: "Digital Marketing Agency in Dhaka | Umbrella Corp HQ."

Meta Descriptions

The meta description appears under the title tag in search results. It doesn't directly affect rankings but significantly impacts click-through rates. Write a compelling 150–160 character description that includes your primary keyword and a clear reason to click.

Heading Structure (H1, H2, H3)

Each page should have exactly one H1 tag containing your primary keyword. Use H2 tags for major sections and H3 for subsections. This structure helps Google understand your page's content hierarchy. Never skip heading levels or use headings purely for visual styling.

URL Structure

Clean, descriptive URLs rank better and get more clicks. Use hyphens to separate words, include your target keyword, and keep URLs short. Example: yoursite.com/services/seo-bangladesh instead of yoursite.com/page?id=47.

Image Optimisation

Every image needs descriptive alt text that includes relevant keywords. Image files should be compressed (WebP format preferred) to reduce page load time. Compress all images to under 100KB without visible quality loss using tools like TinyPNG or Squoosh.

Internal Linking

Link from your high-traffic pages to your target pages. If your "About Us" page gets traffic, add a link to your main service page. Internal links pass ranking authority between pages and help Google discover and index your content.

Fix #4: Create Content Your Customers Actually Search For

The Bangladesh businesses that dominate Google aren't necessarily the oldest or the best-funded — they're the ones that have built the most useful, relevant content for their target audience. Content is how you rank for keywords that your main service pages can't capture.

Content Types That Work for Bangladesh Businesses

  • Location-specific service pages: If you serve multiple areas, create dedicated pages for each. "Digital Marketing Agency Dhaka," "SEO Services Chittagong," "Social Media Management Sylhet." These rank for hyper-local searches.
  • FAQ pages: Compile the 10–20 most common questions your customers ask before buying. Answer them thoroughly. These pages often appear in Google's "People Also Ask" boxes, giving you additional visibility.
  • Industry-specific guides: "How RMG factories can use digital marketing to find buyers," "SEO for Bangladeshi e-commerce businesses," "Social media marketing for Bangladesh restaurants." These highly specific pieces rank for niche keywords with low competition and high intent.
  • Comparison content: "Best digital marketing agencies in Dhaka compared," "Facebook Ads vs Google Ads for Bangladesh businesses." Comparison content targets users who are actively researching before making a decision — the highest-intent stage.

Fix #5: Fix Your Page Speed — Bangladesh Mobile Stats

Bangladesh has 91% mobile internet usage. Most of those users are on 4G connections with variable quality. A website that loads in 2 seconds in a Dhaka office on WiFi might take 8 seconds on a mobile connection in Cumilla. Page speed is both a ranking factor and a conversion factor — every additional second of load time reduces conversions significantly.

Diagnosing Your Page Speed

Use Google PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev) or our free Website Health Checker to audit your current page speed. Aim for a score above 80 on mobile. Most Bangladesh business sites score below 40.

The Most Impactful Speed Fixes

  1. Compress and convert images to WebP. Oversized images are responsible for the majority of slow load times. A single uncompressed image can be 3–5MB; the same image in WebP can be under 100KB with no visible quality difference.
  2. Upgrade your hosting. Many Bangladesh businesses use shared hosting at ৳500–1,000/month. The performance difference between cheap shared hosting and a ৳2,000–4,000/month VPS or quality shared hosting plan is enormous. For WordPress sites, consider Cloudflare's free CDN — it serves your content from servers closer to your visitors worldwide.
  3. Enable browser caching and compression. Most quality hosting providers offer Gzip/Brotli compression in the control panel. Browser caching stores static files locally on visitors' devices so returning visitors load your site almost instantly.
  4. Reduce render-blocking scripts. Load JavaScript asynchronously and defer non-critical scripts. This prevents external scripts from blocking your page from displaying.
  5. Use a lightweight theme (WordPress users). Many Bangladeshi WordPress sites run on bloated theme frameworks that load dozens of unnecessary libraries. Switch to a lightweight theme like GeneratePress or Astra.

Fix #6: Build Local Backlinks

Backlinks — links from other websites to yours — remain one of Google's strongest ranking signals. A website with 50 quality backlinks will almost always outrank an otherwise identical site with zero backlinks. Building backlinks in the Bangladesh market is actually easier than in more competitive markets because very few local businesses are doing it at all.

Backlink Building Strategies for Bangladesh Businesses

  • Local business directories: List your business on Yellow Pages Bangladesh, Bikroy.com, bdpages.com, and other local directories. These provide authoritative local citations that strengthen your Google Business Profile and website rankings simultaneously.
  • Chamber of Commerce and trade associations: DCCI (Dhaka Chamber of Commerce), BGMEA, BASIS, and other industry associations typically link to member business websites. Membership often comes with a directory listing — a high-authority backlink.
  • Local news and media: Get featured in The Daily Star, Prothom Alo, The Financial Express, or industry publications. Send press releases for business milestones, events, or unique stories. A single mention in a major Bangladeshi news outlet is a powerful backlink.
  • Guest posting on local blogs: Write guest articles for other Bangladesh business blogs, industry publications, and news sites. Include a natural link back to your website within the content.
  • Partner and supplier links: Ask your business partners, suppliers, and clients to link to your website from theirs — in a testimonials section, partner page, or relevant blog mention. These are often the easiest backlinks to acquire.
  • Create link-worthy content: Publish original research, industry surveys, or unique data about the Bangladesh market. Content that provides unique, citable data naturally attracts backlinks from journalists, bloggers, and researchers referencing your findings.

The 90-Day Bangladesh SEO Action Plan

Here is a prioritised, actionable 90-day SEO roadmap specifically designed for Bangladesh businesses starting from scratch or from a weak baseline:

Days 1–30: Foundation

  1. Claim and fully optimise your Google Business Profile — this alone can move you into the Local Pack within weeks.
  2. Install Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console. You cannot improve what you don't measure.
  3. Run a full site audit using our Website Health Checker or Google Search Console's Coverage report. Fix crawl errors, broken links, and missing title tags.
  4. Optimise title tags and meta descriptions for your five most important pages: homepage, main service page, about page, contact page, and your highest-traffic blog or content page.
  5. Compress all images across your site and test your PageSpeed Insights score. Fix the top three speed issues.
  6. List your business on the top 10 Bangladesh local directories.

Days 31–60: Content and Keywords

  1. Complete keyword research: identify 20–30 priority keywords across your core services and locations. Map each keyword to a specific page.
  2. Create or update location-specific service pages for each area you serve.
  3. Publish 4–6 blog posts targeting long-tail question keywords your customers search for. Focus on local relevance — mention Bangladesh, Dhaka, or your specific city specifically.
  4. Start a review acquisition system: ask every satisfied customer for a Google review. Aim for 10+ new reviews in this period.
  5. Begin outreach for 5–10 local backlinks: directory listings, partner sites, Chamber of Commerce.

Days 61–90: Authority Building

  1. Analyse your Search Console data: which keywords are you ranking for positions 5–20? These "near-miss" keywords need targeted content updates or new supporting cluster posts to push them to page one.
  2. Publish 4–6 more blog posts building out your topic clusters.
  3. Pursue 3–5 higher-authority backlinks: local press coverage, industry association features, or guest posts on relevant Bangladesh publications.
  4. Add schema markup to your key pages (LocalBusiness, FAQ, Service). Schema helps Google display rich results like star ratings, FAQs, and business information directly in search results.
  5. Review your Google Business Profile insights: which searches are finding your listing? Use this data to further optimise your profile and website content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take for Bangladesh businesses?
For local Bangladesh keywords with low competition, you can see first-page rankings within 60–90 days with proper optimisation. National Bangladesh keywords take 3–6 months. More competitive terms in broader niches can take 6–12 months. The Google Business Profile Local Pack can show results even faster — sometimes within 2–4 weeks of full optimisation.
Is Google the main search engine in Bangladesh?
Yes — Google holds over 97% of the search market in Bangladesh. Bing and other search engines collectively account for less than 3% of searches. Optimising for Google is effectively optimising for all search in Bangladesh.
How much does SEO cost in Bangladesh?
Basic local SEO starts from ৳8,000–15,000/month covering Google Business Profile optimisation, on-page fixes, and local citations. Full-service SEO including content creation and link building typically runs ৳25,000–60,000/month. DIY SEO using free tools (Google Search Console, Keyword Planner) is possible for ৳0–2,000/month if you invest the time to learn and implement it yourself.
What keywords should Bangladesh businesses target?
Start with location-specific keywords: "your service + Dhaka/Bangladesh" (e.g., "web design agency Dhaka"). Target both English and Bangla keywords to capture the full search market. Long-tail keywords with clear local intent — "best accountant in Gulshan Dhaka" — convert far better than broad national terms and are easier to rank for.
Do I need a Bangla website for Bangladesh SEO?
Having Bangla content significantly improves local relevance and helps rank for Bangla-script search queries, which represent a large and growing share of Bangladesh mobile search. A bilingual site (English + Bangla) is ideal for maximum search coverage. At minimum, your Google Business Profile description should include both languages.
