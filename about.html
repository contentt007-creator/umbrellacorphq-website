About Us

The Corp Behind
The Coverage.

We are a full-service marketing and branding agency based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We build brands, run campaigns, and grow businesses — without the fluff.

Our Story

Built In Dhaka.
Built For Results.

Umbrella Corp HQ was founded on a simple observation: most marketing agencies in Bangladesh were either too expensive, too slow, or too detached from real business outcomes. They ran ads without strategy, designed brands without positioning, and built websites without thinking about conversion.

We built Umbrella Corp HQ to be the agency we wished existed. One team that handles every layer of marketing and branding under one roof, with one strategic brief, and one standard: measurable results.

Since founding, we’ve worked with brands across food and beverage, fashion, retail, real estate, and professional services. We serve clients in Dhaka, across Bangladesh, and internationally. Every project we take on is treated like our own brand is on the line.

Mission

To Make Great Marketing Accessible.

World-class branding and marketing strategy should not be the exclusive domain of multinationals with unlimited budgets. We bring the same strategic rigour and creative standards to every project, regardless of client size.

Vision

Bangladesh’s Most Trusted Marketing Partner.

To be the agency that Dhaka’s best brands choose, and the agency that international clients choose when they want to enter Bangladesh. Known for results. Respected for integrity.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Results First

We are judged by what we deliver, not what we pitch. Every decision we make is measured against one question: does this move the business forward?

Radical Honesty

We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. If your brief is wrong, we’ll say so. If a campaign is underperforming, we’ll tell you why before you ask.

Strategic Precision

We don’t throw things at the wall. Every tactic is chosen because it’s the right tool for the specific job. Good strategy means fewer activities, better executed.

Long-term Thinking

We don’t optimise for the short-term at the expense of the long-term. Brands built on solid foundations outlast brands built on trends. We build for both.
